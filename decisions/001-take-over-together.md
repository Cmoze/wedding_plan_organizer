# Decision 001: Take over Together after the hackathon

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Status:** Active

## Context

Together was built at the fall hackathon by three contributors. Kai and Caleb have stepped
back, and Carter is taking it forward as their product for MSB 341, starting in Sprint 4.
The full record, including why the dental admin product it replaces was paused, is Decision
007 in Cmoze/cmoser0_msb341:
`decisions/007-pause-dental-take-on-wedding-planner.md`.

## Options considered

1. **Keep going on the dental checker**: its next steps all need purchases (the HIPAA plan
   and BAA, the Dentrix developer fee), and no office would beta test it.
2. **Take over Together** (chosen): a live app with an AI planner to measure, and testers
   within reach.
3. **Start a new idea from scratch**: a sprint or more of discovery before anything to test.

## Decision

Take over Together. The deciding reason: it can be tested and improved with what's on hand.
From here on, this repo holds Together's discovery, design, decisions, specs, GTM, and evals
alongside the code. Sprint plans and reviews stay in Cmoze/cmoser0_msb341.

## What would change our mind

Sprint 4's tests with 5 couples and parents show no one would choose Together over Zola or
The Knot.
